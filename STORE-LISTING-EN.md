## Long description (4000 chars max)

```
That person you just met at a party: five-star hotel or wild camping?
Your best friend, the one you think you know inside out: sushi or pineapple on pizza?

Snap Tap is the game that puts your certainties to the test. Each round, one player says whether they want what they LOVE or what they HATE. Everyone else plays the card they think fits them best. They pick their favorite without knowing who played it. If it's yours, you score. If not, maybe you don't know them as well as you thought.

🎮 HOW IT WORKS
• Create a room, your friends scan the QR code
• Everyone plays on their own phone
• 3 to 16 players, games of 15 to 30 minutes
• No account needed, you're playing in 30 seconds

👍 LOVE, 👎 HATE
Read the others' tastes to play the right card at the right time. First to 5 points wins.

🃏 300+ CARDS, 20 CATEGORIES
Food, Movies, Music, Travel, Work, Absurd... categories for every mood, switched on by the room host.

⚡ SPECIAL ROUNDS & REACTIONS
Double round, timed round, swap round: twists pop up to spice things up. And everyone reacts live during the reveal.

🍻 PARTY MODE (drinking game)
Turn the game into a drinking game: every chosen card triggers a rule tied to its subject, plus challenges and votes. The boss and the winner never drink, that's the reward. Please drink responsibly, a non-alcoholic drink works just as well.

🏆 WIN COUNTER
Run the rematches, the trophy tracks the night's wins.

No app? Your friends can join from their browser. But it's better with the app. 😉
```
